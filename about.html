About Saveria

Identity, translated into space.

A reflection of identity.

ProcessSelecting the palette

SAVERIA translates identity into environment. For clients who have already built something exacting, that identity is not abstract; it's the same instinct that shaped their restaurant, their hotel, their home, now applied to the space itself.

We do not propose from a moodboard. We propose from who someone is, then test that direction against how they actually live and work, until the result answers to the standard they already hold everywhere else.

We design for legacy, not for trend.

Founder & Creative DirectorKrystie Gerada
Founder & Creative Director

Krystie Gerada

Krystie founded SAVERIA in 2026, after completing Tartarun (Michelin Selected 2026), the studio's pilot project. She previously led DPRO Studio for ten years, working with Malta's architects, estate agents, and property developers on projects in Dubai, the Caribbean, and London.

Her work spans generative AI and interiors: campaigns with Maison Meta for Pandora, Dolce & Gabbana Casa, and Zalando, plus imagery for Bortex Fine Tailoring's 60th anniversary.
