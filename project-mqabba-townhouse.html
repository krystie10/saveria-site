Mqabba Townhouse

RestorationMqabba Townhouse

Scope

Full restoration & interior design

Location

Mqabba, Malta

Client

Private residence

Brief

Structure & character, in equal measure

A village townhouse restoration built around one man's love of wine, art, and quality. The brief asked for structure and character in equal measure, a home that could hold a serious collection without feeling like a gallery, and host without feeling staged.

The restoration worked with the townhouse's existing bones, letting original stonework and proportions set the language for what was added. Materials were chosen to age well rather than photograph well, a deliberate stance against the trend cycle.

Throughout, the project stayed anchored to Mqabba's village core: the home reads as continuous with its street, not apart from it.
