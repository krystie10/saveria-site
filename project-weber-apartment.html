Weber Apartment Rehaul

Retirement HomeWeber Apartment

Scope

Full apartment rehaul

Location

Marsascala, Malta

Client

Private residence, retirement home

Brief

Permanence & daily comfort

A retirement home for a couple who split decades between Manhattan and Latin America. The brief was permanence: daily comfort and years of hosting the people they love, in a home meant to be lived in, not just looked at.

The plan was anchored by a bar at its centre, a deliberate decision reflecting how the couple actually entertains, with sightlines and circulation built outward from that one fixed point.

Material choices favoured warmth and durability over spectacle, giving the apartment the quiet confidence of a home settled into its final chapter.
